### About Me

I'm an applied AI engineer focused on **quantitative finance** and **agent systems**.  
I build end-to-end trading infrastructure — research, backtesting, execution, and validation — and have led agentic products from concept to production.

- Currently building **Zen Trading**'s agentic quantamental platform (multi-hop reasoning equity research, FinRL-X stack, Tradfi-Agent, etc.)
- Previously at **FedML**, **Alibaba Cloud PAI** (federated learning & EAS), and **Tencent CV Lab**
- M.S. in Information Technology from **Carnegie Mellon University**
- B.Eng. & B.Mgt. from **Queen Mary University of London** & **Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications**
- Based in the Bay Area, California

> The bet is that agentic systems work best as a rigorous layer over deterministic models, not a replacement for them.
